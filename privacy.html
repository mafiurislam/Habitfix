DATA ETHICS & PRIVACY

Privacy & Cookie Policy

1. Commitment to Privacy

Habit Fix Wellness ("we", "our", or "us"), founded by Raju Raut, respects the privacy of our visitors and clients. We collect only the information reasonably necessary to conduct wellness assessments, communicate regarding consultation bookings, and provide coaching support.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form or complete our Free Wellness Assessment, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, country, and telephone/WhatsApp number.
  • Lifestyle habits: Routine information, typical sleep, hydration, activity level, and wellness goals voluntarily provided by you.

We do NOT collect unnecessary sensitive health records or financial credit card numbers directly on our website.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is strictly used to:

  • Respond directly to your coaching inquiries and assessment submissions.
  • Schedule and confirm online 1-on-1 consultations.
  • Deliver downloadable wellness habit trackers and educational updates if you request them.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party marketers or advertisers.

4. Cookie Policy

Our static website utilizes minimal essential cookies or local storage solely for functional preferences (such as remembering your timezone during consultation booking and selected language). We do not use aggressive tracking cookies or invasive third-party ad networks.

5. Contact Regarding Your Data

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal contact data at any time by emailing us at coach@habitfixwellness.com or messaging Coach Raju at +91 90512 31155.
