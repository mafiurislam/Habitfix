SERVICE 03

The 21-Day Healthy Habit Journey

A structured 3-week immersive framework designed to build awareness, establish daily micro-habits, and create lasting momentum.

Realistic Expectation: 21 days is not a magic formula. Its purpose is to introduce structured habit building and guide you into continuing healthy practices independently beyond the program.
Days 1–3: Routine baseline audit & cue identification
Days 4–7: Anchor hydration and plate balance
Days 8–14: Daily movement flow & routine resilience
Days 15–21: Habit review, graduation & lifelong maintenance
