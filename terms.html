Terms & Conditions

1. Nature of the Coaching Service

Habit Fix Wellness and Coach Raju Raut offer lifestyle coaching, behavioral habit structuring, and nutritional wellness education. Coaching sessions, assessment guides, and educational materials are designed to empower personal habit awareness and are not medical therapy or healthcare treatments.

2. Consultation Bookings & Attendance

Consultation appointments are scheduled in advance according to the client's preferred timezone. Because coach time is reserved specifically for you, clients are requested to provide at least 12 hours notice if an appointment needs to be rescheduled.

3. Transparent Fee Structure & International Payments

In alignment with Section 20 of our Master Plan, all coaching fees, currencies, session lengths, and program deliverables are clearly disclosed before checkout. No hidden costs or mandatory surprises are added. Payment methods for international clients are handled through secure, established payment processors.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

If you enroll in the 21-Day Habit Journey or an individual coaching package and feel within the first 7 days that the coaching philosophy is not aligned with your expectations, you may request a fair, prorated refund of the remaining unused coaching sessions with no complicated formalities.

5. Client Responsibility

Sustainable lifestyle and weight management results require individual action, daily habit consistency, and transparent communication. The coach provides guidance, education, and compassionate accountability; the client maintains personal autonomy and responsibility for their health decisions.
