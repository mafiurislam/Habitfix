BODY & WELLNESS EDUCATION

Understand Your Numbers: Body Metrics Explained

Health is multidimensional. We educate you on commonly discussed wellness measurements so you never feel intimidated or deceived by a single number.

Total Body Weight

The total mass of your body including water, bone, muscle, organs, and fat tissue.

Why It's Measured

Provides a high-level reference point over extended periods for general weight trends.

Limitations

Fluctuates daily by 1–2 kg simply due to water retention, salt intake, digestion, or stress.

Coach Raju's Takeaway

"Never let a single morning scale reading determine your mood or self-worth. Look at 3-week rolling trends, energy, sleep quality, and how your clothes fit."

Body Mass Index (BMI)

A screening ratio of weight relative to height (kg/m²) used in population studies.

Why It's Measured

Offers a quick general demographic classification of weight ranges.

Limitations

Cannot distinguish between lean muscle mass and fat tissue, nor does it account for fat distribution.

Coach Raju's Takeaway

"BMI is a rough screening metric, not a diagnosis of fitness or health. A muscular person can register as 'overweight' on BMI while being metabolically very fit."

Calculate Your BMI Now

Waist Circumference

The circumference around the abdomen measured horizontally just above the hip bone.

Why It's Measured

Strong correlation with abdominal fat and long-term metabolic health risk factors.

What Influences It

Posture, bloating, meal timing, and visceral fat storage patterns.

Coach Raju's Takeaway

"Waistline improvements are frequently one of the most reliable indicators of sustainable habit changes long before the bathroom scale shows dramatic numbers."

Body Fat Percentage

The proportion of total fat mass relative to total overall body weight.

Why It's Measured

Gives a much clearer picture of body composition than scale weight alone.

Limitations

Consumer bioimpedance scales can have substantial variance based on hydration levels.

Coach Raju's Takeaway

"Prioritize building strength and preserving lean muscle through balanced nutrition and daily movement rather than obsessing over decimal points."

Visceral Fat Rating

The internal fat surrounding vital abdominal organs including liver, stomach, and intestines.

Why It's Measured

Important marker for cardiovascular and metabolic balance.

How It Responds

Visceral fat is usually the quickest to respond positively to consistent sleep, hydration, and brisk daily walking.

Coach Raju's Takeaway

"You don't need dangerous fat-burning pills. Gentle daily consistency, real food, and restorative sleep naturally regulate visceral fat."

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

The baseline number of calories your body burns at complete rest just to keep heart, lungs, and brain functioning.

Why It's Measured

Prevents people from making the dangerous mistake of eating too few calories and crashing their energy.

Influenced By

Muscle mass, age, height, thyroid balance, and chronic dieting history.

Coach Raju's Takeaway

"Severe crash dieting suppresses your BMR and makes weight rebound almost certain. We nourish your metabolism so it stays active and healthy."
