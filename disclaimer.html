Important Wellness & Medical Disclaimer

Habit Fix Wellness and Coach Raju Raut provide general wellness education, lifestyle guidance, and habit coaching. Information provided on this website is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

1. Not a Substitute for Medical Care

Wellness coaching does not replace consultation with a qualified medical doctor, registered dietitian, endocrinologist, physiotherapist, clinical psychologist, or other licensed healthcare professional. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read on this website or discussed during coaching sessions.

2. Existing Conditions, Medications & Pregnancy

If you have a diagnosed medical condition (such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, hypertension, renal impairment, gastrointestinal disorders, eating disorders), take prescription medications, or are pregnant or nursing, consult an appropriate licensed healthcare professional before making any significant changes to your diet, fluid intake, physical activity, or daily routine.

3. Individual Results Vary & No Outcome Guarantees

Any client experiences, transformation stories, and testimonials featured on this website represent genuine individual experiences shared with written consent. However, individual results vary widely based on personal consistency, genetics, hormonal health, initial baseline, and lifestyle adherence. Habit Fix Wellness makes no guarantee of specific weight loss, timeline, or physical outcome.

Articles, downloadable habit trackers, hydration logs, and educational resources provided across this platform are for general informational purposes only. They are designed to promote mindfulness and routine awareness in healthy adults.
